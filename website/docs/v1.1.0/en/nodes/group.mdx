# Group `group`

A group container gathers nodes into a child canvas that the enclosing canvas sees as one step: control enters through `in`, the child canvas runs once, and control continues through `done`. Use it to present and review a self-contained piece of work together, such as a sub-plan or the assessment of one region, without turning it into a child workflow with a history of its own.

## Structure

`start_node_id` must point to the container's single internal `group-start`. Every child uses the container ID as `parentId`, and the entry node's `next` port connects to the first executable child. Child edges cannot cross the container boundary; nodes outside read the container's own result. A group may contain an iteration or a loop.

## Group or child workflow

A group describes graph structure and emits no runtime command; the host decides how its child canvas compiles into steps. Use a [child workflow](./child-workflow) when the work needs its own history, state, and cancellation policy, and an [iteration](./iteration) to repeat a child graph over a collection.
